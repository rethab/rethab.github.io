Mosaic

Drop in a picture and this algorithm tries to paint it by generating random images, combine them, and make random mutations. By throwing away bad versions and keeping the good ones, it approximates the image on the left.

Everything happens directly in the browser so selecting an image does not upload it anywhere.

Every generation breeds a full population and diffs each candidate against your image, pixel by pixel. Bigger grids and populations mean fewer generations per second.

Mutation
—
 

How hard each kid gets tweaked after mixing its parents. It tunes itself with Rechenberg's 1/5 rule: if more than 1 in 5 kids beat their parents, tweak more, otherwise tweak less. That's why it shrinks as the picture sharpens.

Best fitness over the run x: % of max generations · y: best fitness

How close the best candidate is to your image, 100% being a perfect copy. Pure noise already scores around 80%, so the interesting stuff happens in the last few percent.
